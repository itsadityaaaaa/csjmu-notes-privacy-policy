Privacy Policy

CSJMU Notes & PYQ helps students access academic notes and previous-year question papers. This policy explains how information and files are handled when you use the app.

2. Information we handle

The app does not use Google Sign-In and does not ask users to sign in with a Google account. Firebase Analytics has not been enabled in the currently released version, according to the developer's current description.

When you use the app's file-submission or academic-material features, the app may handle:

  • Files you submit: notes, PDFs, or other supported academic documents you choose to upload.
  • Submission details: details entered with a file, such as its title, subject, course, description, upload status, and review status, where those fields are available.
  • Technical and operational data: information processed by the app, Android, Google Drive, or infrastructure used to transfer, store, retrieve, or troubleshoot files. The exact data depends on the app's implementation and service configuration.

Please do not upload sensitive personal information or material you do not have permission to share.

3. How we use information

Information and files are used to provide academic resources, receive submissions, show file details and review status, allow an administrator to review submitted material, and make approved material available through the app where that feature is enabled. Technical information may be used to maintain reliability and investigate errors.

4. File storage, administrator review, and sharing

The app uses Google Drive as part of its file-storage or file-delivery workflow. Files and associated metadata sent to Google Drive are processed under Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

Files submitted for approval may be accessed by the app administrator or authorised reviewers for moderation. A submitted file should not be treated as publicly available until it is approved and made available through the app. Approved files may be accessible to app users according to the app's sharing settings.

Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell personal information. Information and files may be processed by Google Drive and the technical services required to operate the app's upload, storage, and download features. The administrator or authorised reviewers may access submissions for review. Approved academic files may be shared with users through the app as described above.

If additional third-party SDKs or services are added in a future version, this policy and the app's Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated as appropriate.

6. Analytics

Firebase Analytics is not currently active in the app version described by the developer. If analytics or other measurement tools are enabled in a future release, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration should be updated to describe the data collected and its use.

7. Retention and deletion requests

Submitted files and related submission information may be retained while they are being reviewed and for as long as needed to provide the app's features, maintain academic resources, address misuse, or meet applicable obligations. Actual retention depends on the app's storage and moderation setup.

To request deletion of a submission or ask a privacy question, email the contact listed in Section 1 and include enough information to identify the file. Requests will be handled for information the developer controls. Copies retained by a third-party provider may also be subject to that provider's systems and processes.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the app. Secure connections should be used when communicating with supported services. No system for storing or transmitting data can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's privacy

The app provides academic resources for students and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Users should submit only files they have the right to share. If you believe personal information has been submitted inappropriately, contact the developer using the email in Section 1.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page, with the effective date updated accordingly.
